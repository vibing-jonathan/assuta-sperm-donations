# Design System: Clinical Helix
**Project ID:** 4570682696187362924

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Clinical, precise, and trustworthy. The interface is deliberately data-first and compact for repeated medical administration work. Right-to-left flow is foundational: navigation is docked on the right, labels are right-aligned, and mixed-direction email addresses remain readable.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Clinical Navy (#0B397C):** Primary actions, active navigation, identity, and focus states.
- **Deep Navy (#002457):** High-emphasis actions and headings.
- **Warm White (#F8F9FB):** Low-glare application background.
- **Pure White (#FFFFFF):** Forms, tables, dialogs, and working surfaces.
- **Soft Slate (#D6DDE8):** One-pixel structure and dividers.
- **Restrained Teal (#087568):** Connected, verified, and positive status.
- **Clinical Red (#A32222):** Destructive actions and validation errors only.

## 3. Typography Rules
Use a highly legible sans-serif stack that supports Hebrew, with compact 14px body copy for dense records. Use 600–700 weight sparingly for page headings, field labels, and table headers. Render donor identifiers and measurements with tabular numerals.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Gently rounded 8px corners and a minimum 44px target. Primary actions use Clinical Navy; secondary actions use a bordered white surface.
* **Cards/Containers:** White surfaces, subtle 1px slate borders, 8–12px corners, and no shadow. Reserve a whisper-soft ambient shadow for dialogs and menus.
* **Inputs/Forms:** 40–44px height, visible labels above inputs, one-pixel slate borders, and a navy border plus soft focus ring on keyboard focus.
* **Data Tables:** The dominant working surface. Use horizontal separators, compact cells, a subdued sticky header, alternating rows, and horizontal overflow.
* **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped with both text and color, never color alone.
* **RTL Dialogs:** Centered with an overlay, close control at the top-left, and explicit save/cancel actions.

## 5. Layout Principles
Use a fixed 256px right sidebar on desktop and a compact top navigation on smaller screens. Keep the workspace fluid and table-first. Follow a 4px baseline with 8px and 16px rhythm, 24–32px page margins, and restrained summary blocks. Avoid decorative imagery, marketing sections, oversized headings, and nested card grids.
